Same application, same record. The flow grows with each new need.

| Step | Content |
|---|---|
| **Starting need** | Caltech must obtain the right to read Léa's record on a protected API. |
| **First question** | How can Caltech obtain that right when Léa uses her browser? → [[01-oauth2/index\|OAuth 2.0]] |
| **Next questions** | How do we secure the code exchange ([[02-pkce/index\|PKCE]]), identify Léa in Caltech ([[03-openid-connect/index\|OpenID Connect]]) and verify her identity according to a policy ([[05-mfa/index\|MFA]])? |
| **By the end of the course** | Explain why each mechanism exists and find its effect in an HTTP trace. |
