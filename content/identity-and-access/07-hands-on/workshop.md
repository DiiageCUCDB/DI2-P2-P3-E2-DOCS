In pairs, choose **one local sign-in and one federated sign-in**.

| Step | Instruction |
|---|---|
| **Annotate** | For each request, note who sends it, who receives it and which data binds the request to its transaction. |
| **Explain** | Find the browser leg and the direct server-to-server exchange. Show where each of the three tokens is used. |
| **Test** | For passkeys, MFA and federation, show one success case and one denial case on Caltech. |
| **Present** | Provide a sequence diagram and traces **without personal data**, with sensitive identifiers, cookies, codes and tokens **masked**. |
